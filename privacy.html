Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how GrowthOps AI Limited ("GrowthOps AI", "we", "us"), a company incorporated in Hong Kong, collects and uses personal information when you visit growthopsads.com or go.growthopsads.com, fill out one of our forms (including Facebook and Instagram lead forms), book a call, buy our services or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: first name, last name, email address, phone number and business website.
  • Your answers to our application questions, such as your business type, how you produce ad creative, your monthly ad spend range and how urgent the need is.
  • Consent records: whether you agreed to receive text messages or calls, which types, and the date, time, form and IP address of that consent.
  • Messages you send us, and notes or recordings of calls (we tell you at the start of a call if it is recorded).
  • If you become a client: billing details (card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number), the materials you send us for your ads, and the ad results you share with us or that we see through partner access to your ad account.
  • Website usage data: our homepage uses the Meta Pixel, which records page visits and similar events so we can measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may use cookies for this.

How we use it

  • To review whether our service is a fit for your business.
  • To contact you by email, phone call, voicemail or text message about your application and to schedule and remind you about calls, as described below.
  • To provide the services you buy, bill for them and support you.
  • To send information about our services that you asked for. You can opt out at any time.
  • To measure and improve our ads and website, keep our records and meet our legal obligations.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and check an SMS consent box on one of our forms, GrowthOps AI may send you text messages about your application and strategy call (follow-up questions, scheduling, confirmations and reminders). If you also check the separate marketing box, we may send occasional messages about our services and offers. Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of purchase.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@growthopsads.com.
  • Full SMS program terms: growthopsads.com/terms.html#sms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We never sell, rent or trade mobile phone numbers or SMS consent.

We use a messaging platform provider only to deliver our own text messages and calls to you. That provider processes your number on our behalf, for that purpose only, and may not use it for its own marketing.

Calls and voicemail

If you check the separate, optional box agreeing to calls, we may call you about your application, your strategy call and our services, including with prerecorded messages and voicemail left without your phone ringing. You can withdraw this consent at any time by telling us on a call, replying STOP to one of our text messages, or emailing hello@growthopsads.com. See section 13 of our Terms.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. Other than as described under Text messages (which always applies to mobile numbers and SMS consent), we share it only with service providers that help us run our business, and only for the purposes above, or when required by law. These include Meta (lead forms, the Meta Pixel and running ads), our CRM, which also handles our forms, calendar, email, text messages and calls, Stripe (payments), Google Workspace (email), a call note-taking tool, and the AI tools we use to produce ads (which receive your product materials, not your contact details).

Where it is stored

Our service providers may store and process information outside Hong Kong, including in the United States. We use providers that protect personal information with appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we are in contact with you and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer. Billing and business records for clients are kept as long as Hong Kong law requires. We keep a record of opt-outs so that we do not contact you again.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. Reply "STOP" to any text message, use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us using the details below. You can limit ad cookies through your browser settings and your Facebook ad preferences. We handle requests in line with Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and other laws that apply to you.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
