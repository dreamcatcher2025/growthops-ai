FAQ

Do AI ads look fake?

Some do. We don't make fake testimonials or synthetic "customers". We make animation, characters, street-interview formats and the founder's own disclosed avatar. Those are formats a creator pool can't produce, and they don't depend on pretending to be real.

Will this get my ad account in trouble?

No. Claiming a synthetic person is a real customer is deceptive advertising. We never do that. Dramatizations are labeled, avatars are disclosed, and Meta's AI label is not a ranking penalty.

How fast do I get the first ads?

Your first 3 ads are free and delivered within a week of the call. After that, 5 new ads per round, 3 rounds a month.

Do I need to film anything or ship product?

No. No studio, no creators, no samples. If you want to be in the ads yourself, we build your avatar from one recording session and you approve scripts from then on.

Who owns the ads?

You do. Run them anywhere, as long as you like.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. The paid plan is disclosed before you commit, and you see the first 3 ads before you pay anything.

Can't I do this with a $50 AI tool?

You can generate clips with one. The tool is the commodity. Angle research, scripting from your data, QA and the weekly loop are the work, and that's what you're paying for.
